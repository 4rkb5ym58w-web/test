## Intent

Snapchat ad for Sabai Sabike, a scooter & motorbike rental company in Phuket.
Professional motion-design look that builds trust with people about to arrive on
holiday, so they reserve a scooter in advance. Opens on a WhatsApp conversation
(user's request), then shows the real fleet, then a WhatsApp booking end card.

## Assets

- assets/fleet/*.jpg — the owner's real fleet photos (NMAX 155 blue/black, ADV 160 black/green, MT-07), cropped to remove the baked-in red labels; a background car plate is blurred.

## Notes

- No unverified claims (no prices, insurance, delivery or fleet size): only the models shown, WhatsApp booking, Phuket.
- WhatsApp number not supplied yet — the end card uses a "Book on WhatsApp" button only; the Snap CTA links to WhatsApp.
- Keep the bottom ~300px clear of key content for Snapchat's CTA overlay.
- Silent music bed: SFX only (no music provider available); a trending sound can be added in Snap Ads Manager.
